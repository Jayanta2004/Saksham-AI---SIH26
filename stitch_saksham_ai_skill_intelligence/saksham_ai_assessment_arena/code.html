In a two-stage stratified sampling design where Census Villages are FSUs and Households are SSUs, what is the primary role of the multiplier?

MoSPI Methodological Rationale

In NSSO surveys, the multiplier is essentially the inverse of the probability of selection. In a two-stage design, the overall probability of selecting a household is the product of the probability of selecting the FSU (village) and the conditional probability of selecting the SSU (household) given the FSU is selected.

Multiplier (M) = 1 / (P(FSU) × P(SSU | FSU))
Source: NSS Survey Design Guidelines, Section 4.2
